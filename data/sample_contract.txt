SUPPLY AND SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made between Northwind Pty Ltd (the "Client") and
Cobalt Systems Pty Ltd (the "Supplier").

ARTICLE III - PAYMENT

3.1 Payment Terms. The Client shall pay all undisputed invoices within thirty
(30) days of receipt. Payment obligations under this clause are subject to
clause 14.2.

3.2 Late Payment. Interest accrues on overdue amounts at 2% per month,
compounding monthly, from the due date until payment is received in full.

ARTICLE VII - LIABILITY

7.4 Limitation of Liability. Notwithstanding any other provision of this
Agreement, the Supplier's total aggregate liability arising out of or in
connection with this Agreement shall not exceed the fees paid by the Client
in the three (3) months immediately preceding the event giving rise to the
claim. This clause does not limit liability under clause 12.3.

ARTICLE IX - TERM AND TERMINATION

9.1 Termination for Convenience. The Supplier may terminate this Agreement at
any time on fourteen (14) days written notice to the Client, without cause and
without liability, subject to clause 7.4.

9.2 Termination for Breach. Either party may terminate immediately on written
notice if the other party commits a material breach that is not remedied
within ten (10) business days.

ARTICLE XII - INDEMNITY

12.3 Indemnity. The Client shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless the
Supplier against any and all claims, losses, damages and expenses arising from
the Client's use of the deliverables, howsoever caused, including where such
claims arise in part from the Supplier's own negligence.

ARTICLE XIV - DISPUTES

14.2 Disputed Invoices. Where the Client disputes an invoice in good faith and
notifies the Supplier in writing within ten (10) business days of receipt, the
payment obligation in clause 3.1 is suspended in respect of the disputed
amount pending resolution.

14.5 Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of New South Wales,
Australia, and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of its courts.
